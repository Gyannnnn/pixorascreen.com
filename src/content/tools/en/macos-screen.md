## How to Set Up the Fake macOS Update

When a Mac installs an update, the screen goes dark, the Apple logo sits in the middle, and a thin progress bar fills slowly underneath with an estimate of the time remaining. This page recreates that screen in the browser, so any Mac, or any other computer, appears to be installing macOS.

1. Open the settings and choose a **Theme**: macOS Dark, macOS Light, or Space Gray.
2. Choose a **Mode**: Realistic, Timer, Manual, or Infinite.
3. Set a **Duration** (5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes or 1 hour) for Timer mode, or a **Speed** (1 to 10).
4. Pick a **Scenario** for the status messages.
5. Press <kbd>F</kbd> for fullscreen, <kbd>Space</kbd> to pause, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> to stop.

## What Makes It Convincing

Mac updates are quiet and minimal, so small details matter. The progress bar moves in uneven steps, with long pauses at stages like "Checking system volume…" and "Cleaning up…", and the time estimate changes as it goes, from "About 15 minutes remaining" to "About 1 minute remaining" that lingers. Realistic mode reproduces that rhythm. On a MacBook, the dark theme in fullscreen is indistinguishable at a glance from the real thing.

## Ideas for Using It

- **Office pranks:** leave a colleague's Mac showing an update that runs for an hour, then reveal it with a press of Esc.
- **Video and theatre:** a believable Mac screen for a scene, with Manual mode to set the exact progress.
- **Teaching:** show students or new staff what a normal update looks like and why it should not be interrupted.
- **A visible pause:** an "updating" Mac makes it clear you are away for a few minutes.

## If a Real Mac Update Is Stuck

A real update can stay at "About 1 minute remaining" for far longer than a minute. Wait at least an hour. If nothing moves, hold the power button for about ten seconds, then turn the Mac on again; the installer usually continues. If it keeps failing, start up in macOS Recovery (hold the power button on Apple silicon Macs, or Command-R at startup on Intel Macs) and choose Reinstall macOS, which keeps your files and apps.
