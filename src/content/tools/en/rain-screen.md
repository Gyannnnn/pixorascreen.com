## Rain on Demand

Some people work best to the sound of rain; others fall asleep to it. The rain screen gives you both the sight and sound of a rainy day on any display: drops falling over a background scene, or rain running down a window pane, with a steady rain soundtrack.

1. Press <kbd>F</kbd> for fullscreen.
2. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to open the controls.
3. Choose falling streaks or rain on glass, a background scene, and a color mood.
4. Pick a soundtrack and set the volume, or mute it.
5. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> again to hide the controls.

## Making It Feel Right

- **Rain Drops Intensity** runs from a light drizzle at 10 drops to a downpour at 600. A moderate setting looks the most natural.
- **Wind Slope** tilts the rain from almost vertical to a hard sideways angle, 5° to 60°. A small angle feels calm; a steep one feels stormy.
- **Color moods** tint the rain and light: Pure Water, Glacier Teal, Emerald Forest, Electric Violet, Sunset Amber, Muted Slate, Crimson Storm, and Cyber Neon Green.
- **Glass mode** places droplets on a window pane and softens the scene behind it, the coziest setting for a rainy evening indoors.

Press <kbd>M</kbd> to toggle the sound, and <kbd>R</kbd> to reset everything to the defaults.

## Rain for Focus

A steady rain sound is one of the most popular backgrounds for studying and deep work. It is constant, has no words to pull your attention, and covers sudden noises that would otherwise break concentration. Keep the volume just loud enough to cover the room's background noise; louder is not better for focus. On a second monitor, the falling rain adds slow movement at the edge of your vision without asking you to look at it.

## Rain for Sleep and Relaxation

For bedtime, use glass mode with a dark scene, lower the volume, and dim your screen. The page has no sleep timer, so set your phone, tablet, or TV to switch the screen off after a while. If you prefer sound without any picture, the [white noise screen](tool:white-noise-screen) offers white noise and a deeper pink noise, which many people find similar to rain.

## Keeping the Volume Comfortable

Background sound works best when it is barely louder than the room. As a rule of thumb, you should be able to talk over it in a normal voice. For babies and young children, the American Academy of Pediatrics has advised keeping sleep-sound machines well away from the crib and at a low volume, under 50 decibels, about the level of a quiet conversation. On phones and tablets, set the device volume first and then fine-tune with the page's own slider, so a notification sound cannot suddenly blast through at full volume.
