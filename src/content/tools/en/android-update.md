## How to Set Up the Fake Android Update

When an Android phone installs a system update, the screen shows the green Android robot, the words "Installing system update", and a progress bar. This page recreates that screen, ending with the restart animation, so a phone, tablet, or laptop looks as if it is in the middle of an update.

1. Open the settings and choose an **Update time**, from 1 minute to 1 hour.
2. Set a **Start time** if you want the bar to begin part-way, up to 90%.
3. Press <kbd>F</kbd> or the Fullscreen button.
4. Hand over the device, or leave it face up on a desk.
5. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or swipe out of fullscreen, to end it.

## The Update Sequence

The simulation runs in three stages, the same order a real phone follows:

| Stage | What appears |
|---|---|
| Installing | The Android robot, "Installing system update", and a filling progress bar |
| About to restart | "Your device will restart shortly" once the bar reaches 100% |
| Restarting | "Restarting..." and then "Starting Android..." with animated dots |

A short update time suits a quick joke; an hour-long update suits a phone left on a colleague's desk over lunch.

## Keeping the Phone Awake

A phone that dims and locks halfway through the update gives the joke away. Before you start, raise the screen timeout to its longest setting, usually under Settings, Display, Screen timeout, and keep the phone on charge for long updates. Turn the brightness to a normal level: real update screens are not unusually bright, and a dim screen hides the browser's edges better.

## Real Android Updates, Briefly

Real system updates on Android usually download in the background and install when you restart, which is why the installing screen appears only for a few minutes on most phones. Many newer phones install updates onto a second, inactive system partition while you keep using the phone, so the restart itself is quick. Keep the battery above about half, or the charger connected, before installing, because most phones refuse to start an update on a low battery.
