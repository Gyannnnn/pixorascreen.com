## A Fish Tank Without the Upkeep

The aquarium screen fills your display with an animated tank: fish of different sizes swimming at their own pace, light filtering down through the water, and an optional underwater soundscape. It brings the calming effect of an aquarium to a TV, a spare monitor, or a tablet, with no water changes, no filters, and no fish to feed.

1. Press <kbd>F</kbd> for fullscreen.
2. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to open the settings panel.
3. Pick one of four water themes: Lagoon Turquoise, Deep Sea Reef, Abyssal Zone, or Coral Sunset.
4. Set how many fish swim in the tank, from 3 to 26, and how fast they move.
5. Turn on the underwater sound and adjust its volume, or leave it silent.

## Focus Overlays

The aquarium can do more than decorate. Under Marine Focus Overlays, it can show:

- **A clock**, toggled with <kbd>C</kbd>, so a TV or second screen shows the time over the fish.
- **A breathing guide**, toggled with <kbd>B</kbd>, which leads you through box breathing: in for four seconds, hold for four, out for four, hold for four.
- **A focus timer** that starts at 25 minutes, the length of a classic Pomodoro work session.

Because the fish keep moving behind the overlay, it is a gentler way to time a work block or a short break than a bare countdown.

## Choosing a Theme

Each theme changes the color of the water and the light. **Lagoon Turquoise** is bright and tropical, good for daytime and waiting rooms. **Deep Sea Reef** is a richer blue with more contrast. **Abyssal Zone** is dark and quiet, suited to evenings and dark rooms. **Coral Sunset** has warm tones that suit a living room at dusk. On an OLED TV, the darker themes are easiest on the panel during long sessions.

## Where It Works Well

- **Waiting rooms, clinics, and receptions:** a calm screen that needs no maintenance and does not invite reading.
- **Children's rooms:** a tank to watch before bed, with the sound low and the screen dimmed.
- **Home offices:** fish on a second monitor, with the clock overlay on, as a quiet companion during work.
- **Cafés and shop windows:** gentle movement that draws the eye without advertising anything.
