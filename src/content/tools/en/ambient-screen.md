## A Calm Screen for Focus and Rest

The ambient screen fills your display with soft color gradients that slowly flow into one another, with a scattering of stars drifting over them. Nothing on it needs reading or reacting to. It is designed as a background: for a second monitor during deep work, a TV during a quiet evening, or a laptop beside you while you study or meditate.

1. Press <kbd>F</kbd> for fullscreen.
2. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to open the control panel.
3. Choose one of six color moods: Cyber Neon, Aurora Dreams, Sunset Solitude, Deep Forest, Cherry Blossom, or Cosmic Myst.
4. Set **Morphing Flow Speed** for how fast the colors move, from still to lively, and **Cosmic Star Density** for how many stars drift across.
5. Mix in sound if you want it, then press <kbd>Space</kbd> to hide the panel.

## Sound Mixer

The Atmospheric Soundscape Mixer gives each sound its own volume, so you can build the background you like:

- **Ambient music tracks:** slow, wordless pads that suit reading and writing.
- **Calming lo-fi synth:** a soft, steady loop for studying.
- **Gentle rainfall:** broadband rain that masks distracting noise.
- **Crackling hearth:** a wood fire's soft pops and hiss, warmer than rain for evening reading.

Quiet rain under a little music works well for focus; rain alone at a low volume suits falling asleep.

## Focus Overlays

Three overlays turn the ambient screen into a quiet productivity tool:

- **Clock** (<kbd>C</kbd>): the time in large type over the gradients, with a choice of fonts, handy on a TV or a spare monitor.
- **Breathing guide** (<kbd>B</kbd>): a circle that leads you through box breathing, four seconds in, four held, four out, four held.
- **Focus timer:** a countdown for a work session, 25 minutes by default, with a ring that fills as time passes.

The gradients keep moving behind each overlay, which makes a long countdown feel less stark than a bare clock face.

## Getting the Most From It

Choose darker moods such as Cosmic Myst or Deep Forest for evenings and for OLED screens, where dark areas keep the pixels nearly off. Brighter moods such as Cherry Blossom or Aurora Dreams suit daytime rooms. Lower the flow speed if the movement catches your eye while you work; the background should fade into the edge of your attention.

For a single steady color instead of moving gradients, the [custom color screen](tool:custom-color-screen) fills the screen with any shade. The [aquarium screen](tool:aquarium-screen) offers the same clock and breathing overlays over a fish tank, and the [stars screen](tool:stars-screen) is the simplest night-sky option.
