## Purple Light for Rooms, Desks, and Streams

A full purple screen is one of the easiest ways to get the violet glow of LED-strip setups without buying anything. This page uses `#7C3AED`, a vivid violet that reads as purple on almost any display. Open it on a TV, a spare monitor, or a tablet, press <kbd>F</kbd> for fullscreen, and the screen becomes a large, soft light source.

- **Gaming and desk setups:** a second monitor or a TV behind the desk washes the wall in violet. Lower the brightness slider for an evening glow that does not compete with your main screen.
- **Streaming backdrop:** a purple screen behind you in a webcam shot gives the scene a consistent brand color, and pairs well with a cool [blue screen](tool:blue-screen) or a warm [pink screen](tool:pink-screen) on the other side.
- **Parties and Halloween:** a laptop or TV in purple instantly sets a spooky or nightclub mood.
- **Photos and video:** violet light from the side gives portraits and product shots a stylized, synth-wave look.

## Choosing a Shade

Purple sits between red and blue, and small changes shift its mood a lot. Toward blue, it feels cooler and more electric; toward red, it becomes magenta and feels warmer. Use the HSL field to explore: keep saturation and lightness the same and move the hue between about 260 (violet) and 300 (magenta). Save the shades you like with the Favorite button, and download any of them as a PNG wallpaper in landscape, portrait, square, or your screen's own size.

## A Purple Screen Is Not a Black Light

Black lights work by giving off ultraviolet light, which makes certain paints, fabrics, and stains fluoresce. Phone, laptop, and TV screens emit no ultraviolet at all. A purple screen only casts visible violet, so it will not reveal stains, check banknotes, or make neon paint glow. For those you need a real UV lamp.

## When Your Screen Turns Purple on Its Own

If every window and photo suddenly has a purple or magenta cast, the screen is most likely missing its green signal. Red plus blue without green looks purple.

1. Reseat the video cable at both ends, then try a different cable and port.
2. Update or reinstall the graphics driver.
3. Reset the monitor to its factory color settings.
4. Open this page and the [green screen](tool:green-screen). If the green screen looks black or very dark, the green channel is not getting through.

On a phone or laptop, purple lines or a purple patch that appears after a drop usually means a damaged display panel rather than a setting. For a full check of each channel, run the [six-color dead pixel test](tool:dead-pixel-test).

To try other colors, the [custom color screen](tool:custom-color-screen) accepts any HEX, RGB, or HSL value.
