## How to Set Up the Broken Screen Prank

The broken screen prank fills a display with a photo of shattered glass, cracks spreading from an impact point, so a perfectly healthy phone or laptop looks as if it has just been dropped. There are eight crack images to choose from, from a small chip in one corner to a fully spiderwebbed screen.

1. Pick a crack from the thumbnails along the bottom of the preview.
2. Click the preview or press <kbd>F</kbd>. The image fills the screen and the browser disappears.
3. Hand the device back, or leave it where its owner will find it.
4. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to reveal the joke.

Clicking or tapping never ends the prank by accident: it only goes into fullscreen, and only <kbd>Esc</kbd> or the back gesture exits.

## Making It Convincing

- **Match the device.** Phones and tablets are the most believable, because they are the devices people actually drop.
- **Pick a sensible crack.** A crack from one corner looks like a fall onto a hard floor; a crack from the middle looks like something hit the screen.
- **Keep it subtle.** Normal brightness, no sound, and a casual "did you drop it?" work better than a dramatic setup.
- **End it quickly.** A broken phone is expensive. Reveal the joke before anyone starts searching for repair shops.

## Why Phone Screens Crack the Way They Do

Modern phone glass is chemically strengthened to resist scratches and everyday knocks, but it is still glass. Most real cracks start at a corner or an edge, because that is where a drop concentrates the force into a small area, and they spread outward in a spiderweb from the impact point. That is why the most believable crack images on this page begin near a corner rather than in the middle of the screen.

## If Your Screen Is Really Cracked

A real crack is worth taking seriously, both for the device and for your fingers:

1. **Check what is damaged.** If the picture is normal, only the outer glass or the screen protector may be cracked. Lines, flickering, black spots, or touch that stops responding mean the display panel underneath is damaged too.
2. **Protect your hands.** Cover the crack with clear tape or a cheap screen protector so glass splinters do not come loose.
3. **Back up your data now.** A cracked screen can fail completely within days, and it is far easier to copy photos and messages while you can still see the display.
4. **Check warranty and insurance.** Many phone plans and card benefits cover accidental damage; an official repair keeps water resistance and features like Face ID working properly.
