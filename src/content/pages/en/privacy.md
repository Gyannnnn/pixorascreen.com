## What the tools themselves collect

Every screen, test, and screensaver runs in your browser. There is no sign-up, login, or upload. The colors you pick, the settings you change, and anything you type into a tool stay on your device.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

PixoraScreen is free because it shows ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

- You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- You can opt out of other third-party vendors' cookies for personalized advertising at [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or, in Europe, [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).
- Google explains how it uses information from sites that show its ads in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent message from Google lets you accept or refuse advertising cookies before they are used. You can change that choice at any time from the same message.

## Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to see aggregate traffic: which tools are opened, which pages need work, and which devices and countries visitors use. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects data such as pages viewed, approximate location, and browser type. We do not use it to identify individual visitors, and we do not sell data. You can block it with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Browser storage

PixoraScreen uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, to remember your own choices:

- Light or dark theme.
- The language you last picked in the language menu.
- Tool settings such as custom colors, speeds, and styles in the screensavers and prank screens.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

## Contact form

If you write to us through the [contact page](page:contact), the name, email address, subject, and message you enter are delivered to our inbox by our email provider, Resend. We use them only to reply to you, and we delete messages we no longer need.

## Hosting and server logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web server, Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. These logs are managed by Cloudflare and kept only for a limited time.

## Children

PixoraScreen is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices and rights

Besides the opt-out links above, you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the tools keep working without them. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you and to have it deleted. Because we hold none beyond contact-form messages, you can make any such request through the [contact page](page:contact).

## Changes to this policy

When the services above change, we update this page and the date at the top.
